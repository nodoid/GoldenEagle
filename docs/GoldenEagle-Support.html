The game

A treacherous lord has stolen the Golden Eagle, the magic statuette that protects the kingdom, and hidden it at the top of his castle. You enter alone, by the gatehouse. Find the eagle and carry it back out through the gate.

Golden Eagle is a new, high-resolution remake of L'Aigle d'Or, the 1984 Oric adventure by Louis-Marie Rocques (Loriciels). Each room is seen in perspective through its open front wall, as in the original. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows, in English and French.

The castle

  • Twenty rooms on four floors: the cellars, the ground floor, the lord's apartments and the eagle's eyrie at the top of the tower. Stairs are marked with an arrow; exits behind you are marked on the floor.
  • Coloured doors (red, green, blue, yellow) are locked. Walk into one carrying the key of the same colour and it opens for good; the key stays in the lock.
  • Dark rooms: without the lamp you can't see objects or traps. Carry the lamp and it lights the room around you.
  • Trapdoors drop you to the floor below, and hurt. One pit in the catacombs has no bottom at all.
  • Ghosts drift towards you and chill you. Carry the cross and they flee. Bats bite; guards in armour push you back.
  • Energy ebbs away slowly. Eat bread to restore it. Potions heal you, except for one colour, which is poison; which one changes every game.
  • You can carry four objects. Treasure (coins, jewels, a goblet, a crown) goes straight into your score.
  • Bring the eagle back out through the gate in the gatehouse to win. The bonus grows with your remaining energy and the speed of your escape.

Questions

A door won't open.

Coloured doors need the key of the same colour, in your inventory. Plain archways and stairs are always open.

I can't see anything.

You're in a dark room. Find the lamp first; until then, feel your way to an exit, and beware of the floor.

How do I change the language?

Choose Language in the menu, or press L on a keyboard. The first time, the game follows your device's language.

Where are my scores kept?

Only on your device, in the game's own storage. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Can I play with a controller?

Yes, on Mac, Windows, iPad, iPhone and Android, with any controller the system recognises.

Privacy

Golden Eagle collects nothing. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it never connects to the internet. It doesn't collect, store, share or sell any personal information, from anyone, including children.

The game keeps two small files in its private storage on your device: the Hall of Fame (the names you enter, with your scores) and your settings (language and volume). They never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.
